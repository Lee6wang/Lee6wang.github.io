Hi! I'm **Haitao Li**, from Hefei, Anhui, China. I received my bachelor's degree in **Mechanical Engineering** from **Hefei University of Technology** in 2025. I am currently on leave from the Master's program in **Instrument Science and Technology** at the School of Instrumentation Science and Opto-electronics Engineering, **Beihang University (BUAA)**, and exploring what comes next.

I remain interested in dexterous robotic hands, embedded systems, and motor control. Through continued learning, hands-on practice, and conversations with others, I hope to gain a clearer sense of what I want to pursue in the long term.

My interests include:

- Dexterous robotic hands and intelligent equipment system design
- Embedded systems and firmware development (STM32 / FreeRTOS / Bootloader)
- Mechatronics and control systems for robotic actuators
- Robotics, including perception, control, navigation, and system integration
- End-to-end hardware-software co-design and engineering implementation

I enjoy understanding systems from the ground up and integrating hardware, firmware, mechanical structures, and control algorithms into reliable and scalable solutions. This website records my engineering practice, technical notes, hands-on projects, and observations and reflections along the way.
